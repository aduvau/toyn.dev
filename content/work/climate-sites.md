## Context

The Agence Parisienne du Climat (Paris's climate agency) and the Clong
(a network of international volunteering organisations) needed content sites
their teams could edit, with accessibility and a low footprint as requirements.

## What I did

- Built the sites in Svelte and SvelteKit on top of DatoCMS, so editors manage
  content without touching code.
- Treated accessibility as a requirement, not a finishing touch.
- Applied eco-design principles — a climate agency's website should not be heavy.

## Outcome

forumhabiterdurable.fr and sportzeroplastique.com for the Agence Parisienne du
Climat, and clong-volontariat.org for the Clong.
